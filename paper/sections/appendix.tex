\section{Radial-velocity measurements}
\label{app:rv_tables}

The full per-star, per-epoch radial-velocity measurements derived from the
\primaryline cross-correlation analysis of Sect.~\ref{sec:methods} are
available as supplementary material upon publication. For each of the
\Ntotal stars in Table~\ref{tab:sample}, the table reports the MJD of
observation, the measured radial velocity, the formal per-epoch
uncertainty $\sigma_\mathrm{RV}$ derived following \citet{Zucker2003},
and the peak-to-peak excursion
$\Delta\mathrm{RV}_\mathrm{max}$ used in the binary classification
(Sect.~\ref{sec:criteria}).
