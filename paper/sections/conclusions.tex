\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We have conducted a spectroscopic multiplicity survey of \Nstars
apparently single WC/WO stars in the Large Magellanic Cloud using
multi-epoch VLT/X-SHOOTER spectroscopy. Our main findings are:

\begin{enumerate}
\item We detect \Ndetected new spectroscopic binaries based on
  radial-velocity variability of the \primaryline emission line, bringing
  the total number of confirmed WC/WO binaries in the LMC to \Nbin out of
  \Ntotal ($\fbinobs$).

\item Through Monte-Carlo bias-correction simulations following the
  methodology of \citet{Dsilva2023}, we constrain the intrinsic binary
  fraction to $f_\mathrm{bin} =
  \fbincorr^{+\fbinerrup}_{-\fbinerrdn}$ and the period-distribution
  exponent to $\pi = \pibestfit^{+\pierrup}_{-\pierrdn}$.

\item The corrected fraction \fbincorr provides the empirical handle on
  the metallicity dependence of WC/WO formation: comparison with the
  Galactic value of \citet{Dsilva2023} tests the prediction that the
  binary channel becomes more important at sub-solar metallicity
  \citep{Shenar2020,Marchant2023}, with the period-distribution exponent
  $\pi$ adding an orthogonal diagnostic of the dominant interaction mode.

\end{enumerate}

Future work will extend the present sample with phase-resolved
spectroscopic monitoring of the \NcandidateBH WC + compact-object
candidates (Sect.~\ref{sec:compact_objects}), full orbital solutions
for the newly detected SB1 systems, and atmospheric analysis of the
WC/WO targets to test for metallicity-dependent wind diagnostics
alongside the binary-fraction measurement reported here.
